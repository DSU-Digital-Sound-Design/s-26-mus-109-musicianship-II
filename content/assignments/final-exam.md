title: "Final Exam Instructions"

A 45-item WebTET listening exam covering the full arc of Quizzes 0–9. One sitting, headphones, our usual classroom setup. Same configurations and audio sources you've practiced with all semester.

Each item plays once — twice for compression and reverb. Write your answer on a numbered answer sheet.

## Item Breakdown

### Section 1 — EQ anchors, single band (16 items)

- **1–3.** Initial anchors (Q0/Q1): 500, 1k, 2k Hz, 1 band, +12 dB, Q ≈ 2, pink noise.
- **4–6.** Reduced gain (Q2): same anchors at +6 dB, music excerpt.
- **7–9.** Expanded octave range (Q3): 250 Hz–4 kHz, +12 dB.
- **10–12.** Boost vs. cut (Q4): 250 Hz–4 kHz, ±12 dB. Name **frequency and direction**.
- **13–16.** Full octave anchors (Q5): 63 Hz–16 kHz, +12 dB, music excerpt only.

### Section 2 — Third-octave neighbors (5 items)

- **17–21.** Q6 level. 500 Hz–2 kHz region (anchors and neighbors: 500 / 630 / 800 / 1k / 1.25k / 1.6k / 2k), +12 dB, Q ≈ 2. Pink noise → music excerpt.

### Section 3 — Two-band EQ (6 items)

- **22–27.** Q7 level. 250 Hz, 500 Hz, 1 kHz, 2 kHz, 4 kHz; **2 simultaneous bands**; +9 to +12 dB; Q ≈ 2; dense music excerpt. Name both boosted bands.

### Section 4 — Compression (8 items)

Q8 level, matching, on a percussive loop.

- **28–31.** 3-attack × 3-release combinations — match attack and release.
- **32–35.** 5-attack × 5-release combinations — match attack and release.

### Section 5 — Reverb (10 items)

Q9 level, dry transient-rich source (voice / snare / pluck).

- **36–38. Decay:** decay varies, mix fixed at 50%, predelay 0. Match decay.
- **39–41. Mix:** decay fixed ≈ 1.2 s, predelay fixed, wet/dry at 3 discrete levels. Match mix.
- **42–45. Predelay:** decay and mix fixed, predelay 0 / ~20 / ~60 ms. Name the predelay bucket.

## How to prepare

Re-run the WebTET presets from each quiz the week before, especially Q6 (third-octave neighbors), Q8 (compression), and Q9 (reverb) — the most recent and hardest. Short, frequent sessions (10–15 minutes) beat one long cram.
